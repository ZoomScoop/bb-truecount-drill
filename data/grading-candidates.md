# Grading Candidates — Pre-Submission Assessment

Working list of cards being evaluated for PSA submission. Each card gets a
visual review (centering, corners, edges, surface) from photos, an estimated
grade range, and a worth-grading call — **this is not an official grade**,
just a read before you spend money on submission + shipping. Compare against
the raw price vs. estimated price at that grade to see if grading clears the
cost (PSA fees + shipping + the multi-month turnaround risk).
